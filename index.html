Questions

Frequently asked questions

Will responses sound automated?

No. Every message is written in your brand voice, and you approve the scripts before launch.

What happens when a question needs a person?

The customer is told a team member will follow up, and the conversation is sent to you immediately. Billing, complaints and special requests always go to your team.

Do I need new software or a new phone number?

No. We connect to the tools you already use wherever possible and handle any setup ourselves.

How long does setup take?

Most systems are live within 7 days of approving the proposal.

Is it allowed to ask customers for Google reviews?

Yes. Google allows asking. It does not allow paying or rewarding people for reviews, or only asking happy customers. The kit asks everyone the same way.

Who owns the accounts and content?

You do. Your profiles, phone numbers, content and customer data remain yours at all times.

Do I need to understand AI?

No. You share how your business runs and approve the work. We handle the technology.
